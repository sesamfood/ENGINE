# Ordering

`/ordering` plans one location at a time. Products with recipe ingredients are hidden by default. Administration's ordering settings store the organization's preference for including them. The list includes the location's selected products and their ingredients. Quantities use each product's default unit. Manual quantities stay separate for each location while the page is open. The planner checks the current date and forecast freshness every minute and when the window regains focus. These updates keep the product list and entered quantities visible while refreshed forecasts load. Export contains positive quantities from the enabled product set, regardless of the search filter.

`ordering.plan` grants access to planning, stock quantities, and ingredient consumption history for sales, Staff food, and Waste. Employee identities and registration details are not returned to the planner. `ordering.export` additionally allows CSV export. Both default to Administrator and Manager. Configured role permissions remain authoritative. Every query checks organization membership and location access. Kiosk mode is excluded.

## Forecast

`lib/ordering-forecast.ts` reads up to 90 complete local calendar days and forecasts each product's consumption quantities independently. The baseline uses the latest 56 days, with a weight that halves every 21 days. With fewer than two open samples for a weekday, it uses the weighted mean across open days. This is a simple seasonal baseline, following the use of seasonal benchmarks described in [Forecasting: Principles and Practice](https://otexts.com/fpp3/simple-methods.html). It has not been calibrated against this organization's future orders.

Customer consumption comes from `salesStockApplications`, the stored ingredient consumption produced by OnlinePOS stock synchronization. No provider API runs when opening the page. Historical recipes, modifiers, and refunds are already represented in these records. Stored quantities are converted to the current default unit using the product's unit mappings. Historical sales entries are not expanded through today's recipes again. Missing conversions are flagged alongside unmapped sales. Removed sales applications are excluded. Pages have bounded reads and load to completion before suggestions or export become available.

The first recorded day is discarded because it may be partial. Subsequent dates without consumption count as zero. A product without positive customer, Staff food, or Waste consumption in its current default unit receives no forecast. Short history and missing stock are flagged. Suggestions pause while stock synchronization is disabled, unfinished, stale, or reports errors. Manual planning remains available. Unmapped sales are also shown. Prepared products may have no consumption of their own because their sales were expanded into ingredients.

The suggested quantity is expected demand for the coverage period, plus the selected buffer, minus positive stock. Negative book stock is treated as zero. Unknown stock is flagged and treated as zero. Suggestions round up to three decimal places in the default unit; pack-size rounding is not inferred from unit names. Manual quantities accept up to six decimal places.

The coverage period starts today and includes lead time. There is no delivery-date model, incoming-order deduction, shelf-life calculation, or automatic purchase submission. Planning changes are held in the open page; download the CSV to keep a completed plan.

## Opening hours, Staff food, and Waste

Sales and Staff food forecasts estimate consumption per scheduled open hour, then multiply by future opening hours. Historical closed dates are excluded from those baselines. Weekly hours, dated exceptions, and the location's operational status are read from existing settings. Overnight hours are split across local calendar dates; overlapping intervals count once. An explicit dated closure overrides overnight carry from the previous day. Closed, temporarily closed, and planned locations have no future customer sales or Staff food demand until their status changes.

Historical opening hours use the saved weekly schedule and dated exceptions. Previous versions of weekly schedules are not stored, so changes to regular hours can affect the interpretation of older data. Scheduled minutes use wall-clock hours. Editing opening hours or operational status invalidates cached sales forecasts and requests an update; ordering reads the current schedule directly. Neither requires weather to be available.

Active `staffFoodRegistrations` and `wasteRegistrations` are loaded separately with bounded pagination. Staff food uses its work date; Waste uses its local registration date. Voided records are excluded. Existing refund-related Waste is read once from the Waste table, alongside net sales consumption. Registration quantities are converted to current default units, and prepared products are expanded through current recipes into ingredients. Missing products or unit conversions are flagged.

Staff food uses its own weekday/hour baseline. Waste uses a separate weekday baseline and can contribute to orders on closed dates because inventory losses can continue while closed. Neither receives a customer weather multiplier or contributes to predicted revenue. The total order forecast is customer consumption + Staff food + Waste, followed by the buffer and one stock deduction. Rows display the Staff food and Waste contributions.

The operational history window is 90 days. Dates without active registrations count as zero, so incomplete registration history can underestimate these amounts. All active recorded Waste is treated as a loss allowance; the app does not distinguish preparation losses from avoidable overproduction. No extra yield percentage or Count discrepancy is inferred. Historical stock deductions are not repeated by the forecast.

## Weather, holidays, and predicted sales

Set `OPENWEATHER_API_KEY` in the Convex deployment's environment settings. Keep it server-side. In Administration, open a location's information and enable weather and holidays. Search for its address, then select a result. This fills the coordinates, country code, and available ISO subdivision code. Existing coordinates remain valid without selecting an address again. Use the location's correct time zone. OpenWeather weather access requires a One Call 4.0 subscription. No sales data is sent to either provider.

Address searches use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service when the user presses Search or Enter. There is no autocomplete request. Searches require location management permission and location access. Results are cached for 30 days, with at most 50 searches per organization. A shared token bucket allows one new provider request every 1.1 seconds across the deployment. Requests identify the application using its configured site origin, and the selector displays OpenStreetMap attribution. `NOMINATIM_SEARCH_URL` can switch to another compatible HTTPS search endpoint without an app update. See the [Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/).

Weather uses OpenWeather's One Call 4.0 daily timeline endpoint, `/data/4.0/onecall/timeline/1day`, documented in the [One Call 4.0 reference](https://openweathermap.org/api/one-call-4.md) and [One Call 3 migration notes](https://openweathermap.org/api/one-call-3-migration.md). Each response page contains 10 daily records, and each page counts as one API call.

Every six-hour refresh fetches yesterday through today plus 27 days in at most three calls. The stored history remains 400 days. If older history is missing, the most recent missing history is requested first, with at most three additional pages per refresh.

A normal warm, steady location uses 12 calls per day. That is 996 calls for 83 locations and 1,008 calls for 84 locations. The initial history needs roughly 40 additional calls per location, spread over about four days. Bootstrap refreshes are capped at six calls per refresh, or 24 calls per location per day. If all 41 locations are bootstrapping, that is 984 calls.

Manual refreshes, failures, gaps, and other apps can add calls. The shared OpenWeather account pool is 1,000 calls across API keys. The default subscription cap is 2,000 calls; set the provider dashboard cap to 1,000 to avoid paid calls.

Weather uses the direct `temp.day` value and daily rain plus snow totals in millimeters, not daily mean temperature. Dates use the location's local calendar and daylight saving time. Unknown weather keeps the existing fallback behavior. Weather attribution: [OpenWeather](https://openweathermap.org/), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Public holidays use the [Nager.Holidays API](https://nagerholidays.com/api). National holidays are included; regional holidays require a matching subdivision code. School holidays, optional observances, and local events are not included. An unavailable calendar is treated as unknown, not as a year without holidays.

`convex/forecasts.ts` refreshes configured locations every six hours and after configuration changes. “Opdatér forslag” also requests a refresh, with ten-minute reuse. Actions fetch providers; queries only read Convex. Each organization/location has a bounded `locationForecasts` document containing at most 428 daily conditions and 28 predicted days. The first refresh after the provider switch records the OpenWeather provider marker and discards the previous provider's weather data and snapshot. Completed archive dates are reused. Weather can be reused for up to five hours within the same local calendar day when all 29 current dates are cached. The cached `weatherWarning` is kept separately from sales warnings and reused with those conditions. The sales calculation still retries when needed, and the six-hour cron refresh normally fetches weather. `weatherUpdatedAt` is persisted independently, so paid historical weather remains available even if the sales forecast calculation aborts. Leases prevent concurrent refreshes and revision checks reject results for changed settings. Provider errors are sanitized before storage.

`lib/sales-forecast.ts` fits weighted ridge regression to log consumption per open hour. Ordering learns weather/holiday effects from each product's consumption quantities. Sales forecasting models the quantity of each external sales product separately, using up to 90 days of stored `salesLines`, grouped by provider and product identifier. Weekday, trend, and annual terms control calendar variation; temperature, precipitation, and public-holiday terms estimate their additional associations with sales. Weather needs at least 56 matched open historical days. Holidays need at least three holiday days, 28 ordinary days, and 56 complete training days. Missing inputs do not become observed weather or holiday values. Forecasting with future explanatory variables follows [Forecasting: Principles and Practice](https://otexts.com/fpp3/forecasting-regression.html).

The model applies those learned effects to the recent weekday baseline, relative to the normal conditions in that baseline. The log offset is 1% of the historical mean hourly demand, so changing between equivalent units such as kilograms and grams does not change the learned effects. Multipliers are bounded between 0.25 and 4. These are practical safeguards, not confidence intervals. Future days outside weather coverage retain their opening-hours-adjusted weekday baseline, with a holiday adjustment if supported. With insufficient history, the model uses the weekday baseline alone. This model has not been backtested against the organization's sales; weather and holiday associations do not establish causation.

Revenue is the sum of predicted product quantities multiplied by their recent weighted realized prices. Price weights halve every 14 days over the latest 56 days. Product histories begin with their first observed positive sale; preceding dates are not treated as zero demand. Prices affect revenue without increasing ingredient quantities. A historical adjustment reconciles line revenue with `salesDaily` over the latest 28 days to account for order-level discounts and charges. Forecasts retain each product's own weekday, weather, and holiday effects, allowing the future sales mix to change.

Product history reads stop at 250,000 lines, 90,000 product/day buckets, or 501 pages per refresh. Incomplete product reads are discarded. Each page and the final save check that the completed sales synchronization has not changed since training began. A running or changed synchronization aborts the refresh and retains the previous snapshot until the next refresh, subject to its normal expiry. Missing or inconsistent product pricing falls back to the aggregate `salesDaily` revenue model with an explicit warning; no partial product total is shown. Only the aggregate 28-day result is stored, keeping location documents bounded. The model version prevents older calculations from being shown after a model change.

The dashboard metric **Forventet omsætning, næste 7 dage** is available in the normal add-widget dialog. It always covers tomorrow through the following six days, regardless of the dashboard's historical date selection. It uses the existing visualizations, currency handling, location selection, sales permissions, and sharing restrictions. It requires at least 14 complete days of synchronized sales. Incomplete location coverage produces a setup/data message instead of a misleading partial total. Stale forecasts expire after 26 hours; missing learned effects show “Begrænset data”. This metric uses the provider-agnostic `salesDaily` history currently populated by OnlinePOS, without adding Wolt revenue a second time.

## Adding data or export formats

`DemandObservation` is independent of the sales provider. Another data adapter can supply dated consumption in a product's default unit. Keep source selection explicit to avoid counting the same sale twice through two providers.

`DemandFactor` accepts a source name, local date, product ID, and nonnegative multiplier. Future event models can supply these factors to `forecastOrder` without changing the ordering UI. Factors multiply predicted customer consumption before Staff food, Waste, the safety buffer, and stock deduction. Weather and holiday effects are already fitted directly to quantities, so do not add them again as external factors. Obtain external data in Convex actions and store it before reading it for a forecast.

`ordering.prepareExport` validates the location, current products, units, quantities, and export permission, then returns format-independent rows. CSV uses the existing escaped, formula-safe, UTF-8 download helper. Future exporters can consume the same rows.
